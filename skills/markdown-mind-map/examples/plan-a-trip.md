# Plan a Trip

- Destination
	- Beach or mountains?
	- Which season is best to visit?
- Set a budget for travel and stays
- Bookings in the right order
	- Flights first, then hotels
	- Rental car for day trips and the airport transfer
	- Restaurant tables
- Travel documents
	- Check passport validity and visa rules for every stop on the route
	- Get travel insurance
- Local tips
	- Ask a friend who has been there
	- Download offline maps before you leave
- Trip journal
	- Every evening write down the highlights
